# Project Charter

## Name

Public project name: **Deliberum**.

Formal process phrase: **quality-centered peer deliberation**.

Internal acronym: **QCPD** may be used in private planning, but public documentation should prefer the phrase rather than the acronym.

## Mission

Deliberum helps multiple heterogeneous participants deliberate around a topic and produce a higher-quality outcome than a single model, fixed role-agent workflow, voting system, or central judge.

## Core goal

The goal is not to simulate human group chat. The goal is to improve the quality of the final outcome.

Quality includes:

- correctness;
- completeness;
- creativity;
- robustness;
- executability;
- explainability;
- traceability;
- uncertainty transparency.

## Non-goals

Deliberum is not:

- a generic chat app;
- a role-agent demo;
- a model voting system;
- a central-Judge pipeline;
- an MCP project;
- a single infinite whiteboard app;
- a workflow engine with a few model calls attached.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. The system starts from a System-issued Topic Contract.
2. Humans, models, tools, and web-only participants are represented as peer Participants.
3. Participation rights are peer-level; conclusion influence depends on argument quality, evidence, objections, and repairs.
4. The first divergence round is sealed to reduce anchoring.
5. The runtime maintains a Candidate Frontier, not a premature single best answer.
6. Candidates are evaluated against explicit Quality Obligations.
7. Objections are first-class objects and cannot be erased by votes.
8. Summaries, rankings, board views, process choices, and final drafts are proposals, not unquestionable truth.
9. The system permits mechanical centers but forbids uncontested semantic centers.
10. Whiteboards, references, addressable objects, adapters, and WebGET are support layers for deliberation quality.
11. The final outcome must include reasons, alternatives, unresolved objections, evidence state, and boundaries.
