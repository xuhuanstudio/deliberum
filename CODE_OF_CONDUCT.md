# Code of Conduct

Deliberum uses the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- being respectful of differing opinions;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, trolling, insults, sexualized language or imagery, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.
